## 2. Hint behavior
| # | Question | Recommendation | Trade-off |
|---|---|---|---|
| H1 | What resets the idle timer? | Any tap on a hotspot or the scene, whether or not it's correct. | Resetting only on correct progress would hint sooner to stuck Users. But a User who is tapping and trying things isn't idle, and a hint could interrupt them. |
| H2 | Which hint level shows after a reset? | Restart at level 1 (vague). | Staying at the current level is more helpful to a stuck User, but earlier levels are then never seen. |
| H3 | After the third hint (45 s)? | It stays visible. Another hint level isn't added. | Cycling through the hints again adds noise. |
| H4 | Hints per story or global? | Scene-wide hints that point toward undiscovered stories: level 1 is vague ("something stirs in the dark"), level 2 names an area (graveyard, patch, house), level 3 gives a clue about the pattern (size, rhythm, order) without stating the trigger. | Per-story hints would need to know which stories were seen, which breaks "stories are independent." Scene-wide hints avoid that, but hints for already-found stories may feel stale. |
| H5 | During a story and while paused? | Timers stop during a story and restart at 0 afterward. They also freeze while paused (the brief requires this). The hint line hides during a story. | None. This follows directly from the brief. |
| H6 | Hint appearance? | One line near the top or bottom, fade in, readable text at 16 px or larger with 4.5:1 contrast. Plain text updates when reduced motion is on. | None |

## 3. Failure behavior
| # | Question | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| F1 | Wrong tap in a sequence | Gives a short distinct "nope" cue (soft sound and a wobble) and resets that sequence's progress. Progress feedback shows the reset. |
| F2 | Progress display | Each correct tap produces a unique tone and a light-up that stays lit until the sequence completes or resets. |
| F3 | Sequence timeout | A partial sequence resets after about 8 s of no taps, so a User can't get stuck in a half-finished state. |
| F4 | Audio blocked or failed to load | The scene still works fully. Events keep visual feedback. A small, non-blocking note says "sound unavailable". Audio unlocks only after Enter, which is a user gesture. |
| F5 | Tapping during a story | Ignored, with no feedback. A story-playing indicator makes clear why. Keyboard focus doesn't move. |
| F6 | Tab hidden or backgrounded | App auto-pauses (the same as Play/Pause), then stays paused until the User resumes. |
| F7 | Reduced motion on | Stories still play, but with fades, glows and sound instead of movement. Ambient motion off. |

Some decisions to make:
- H1 and F3 are the two that most change how it feels to play. Do you want a different timeout than 8 s?
- F6 is a new rule I'm adding: should hiding the tab auto-pause? It's the safer behavior, since sound shouldn't keep playing from a hidden tab, but it's your call.
- Are you comfortable with F7 (stories still play under reduced motion)? The brief requires honoring the preference, but doesn't say stories must be skipped.
